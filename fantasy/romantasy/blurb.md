Book Description Template

Hook/Headline Block

Line 1: Use a high-concept thematic tagline (e.g., three punchy nouns), a "For fans of [Author X, Y, Z]" comparison statement, or a direct warning/command that sets the atmospheric tone immediately.

Line 2 (Optional): Define the core sub-genre tropes (e.g., "A slow-burn, enemies-to-lovers fantasy") or mention social proof like bestseller status.

Body Paragraph 1: The Inciting Incident & The Disadvantage

Sentence 1: Introduce the protagonist, their age (if relevant to the target audience), and their "status quo" or intended life path to establish a baseline of normalcy or a specific disadvantage (poverty, lack of magic, physical fragility).

Sentence 2: Introduce the disruption or inciting incident that forces the protagonist out of their status quo (a kidnapping, a forced conscription, a mistake, or a magical bargain).

Sentence 3: Raise the immediate stakes by clarifying the lethal nature of this new path or the specific reason the protagonist is unlikely to survive it (e.g., "dragons incinerate the weak").

Body Paragraph 2: The Dangerous World & The Romantic Tension

Sentence 1: Describe the setting or "arena" the protagonist must navigate (e.g., a cutthroat court, a war college, a magical realm), focusing on the brutality or treacherousness of the environment.

Sentence 2: Introduce the primary Love Interest (often an antagonist at this stage), characterizing them by their lethality, power, status, or bad reputation.

Sentence 3: Establish the specific dynamic or conflict between the protagonist and the Love Interest (forced proximity, a magical bargain, or an enemies-to-lovers setup) that makes their connection dangerous or forbidden.

Sentence 4: Hint at the complex emotional landscape, suggesting a thin line between hatred, fear, and attraction.

Body Paragraph 3: The Escalation & The Twist

Sentence 1: Pivot to the protagonist's internal resources, contrasting their perceived weakness with a hidden strength, wit, or dormant magical ability that gives them a fighting chance.

Sentence 2: Expand the scope of the conflict from personal survival to a larger external threat (e.g., a failing magical ward, a looming war, a political conspiracy) to raise the stakes.

Sentence 3: Introduce a mystery or conspiracy hook, suggesting that the leadership is lying, a secret is hidden, or nothing is as it seems.

Body Paragraph 4: The Impossible Choice (The Climax)

Sentence 1: Present the ultimate dilemma where the protagonist must choose between two vital things (e.g., survival vs. love, saving their family vs. saving the kingdom).

Sentence 2: Suggest that success will require a total transformation or a sacrifice of who they used to be.

Sentence 3: End with a punchy, high-stakes statement or a rhythmic tagline that summarizes the "do or die" nature of the story (e.g., "Graduate or die," "Save the world or doom it").

Outro Block

Line 1: Reiterate the author’s credentials or connect this book to the previous book in the series (if a sequel).

Line 2: A final call-to-action or genre-positioning statement summarizing the reading experience (e.g., "seductive, breathtaking, and full of twists").
